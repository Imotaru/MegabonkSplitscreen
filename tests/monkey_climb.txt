# Monkey wall climb: P1 and P2 both Monkeys. Each runs at the nearest wall, holds into it, then stops.
chars Monke Monke
wait 2
snap before
shot start
# P1 first
go p1 wall
wait 4
snap p1 at wall
shot p1_wall
wait 2
snap p1 climbing?
stop p1
wait 2
# then P2
go p2 wall
wait 4
snap p2 at wall
shot p2_wall
wait 2
snap p2 climbing?
stop p2
wait 2
snap end
quit
